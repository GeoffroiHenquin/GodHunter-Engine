The idea for the game is to create a FNAF Tycoon game, where the player can build and manage 
their own Freddy Fazbear's Pizza location. The player will be able to design their own animatronics, 
set up the layout of the restaurant, and manage the day-to-day operations. 

Here is a list ideas and potential features for the game:

1. The goal of the game should be to make as much money as possible.
	- The player can earn money by attracting customers, selling food and drinks, and hosting events.
	- The player can also earn money by completing special challenges and objectives.
	- The player can use the money to upgrade their restaurant, buy new animatronics, and unlock new features.
	- The player can loose money in lawsuits, losing reputation, etc.
	- The player can go bankrupt and lose the game if they run out of money.

2. The player should be able to customize their restaurant.
	- The player can choose the layout of the restaurant, including the placement of tables, chairs, and decorations.
	- The player can choose the color scheme and theme of the restaurant.
	- The player can choose the type of food and drinks that are sold in the restaurant.
	- The player can choose the type of animatronics that are used in the restaurant.

3. The player should be able to manage the day-to-day operations of the restaurant.
	- The player can hire and fire employees, including cooks, waitstaff, and security guards.
	- The player can set the prices of food and drinks.
	- The player can set the hours of operation for the restaurant.
	- The player can manage the inventory of food and drinks.

4. The player should be able to design and customize their own animatronics.
	- The player can choose the appearance of the animatronics, including their color, size, and shape.
	- The player can choose the type of animatronics, including Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, Foxy, and others.
	- The player can choose the behavior of the animatronics, including their movement patterns and attack strategies.
	- The player can upgrade the animatronics with new features and abilities.

5. The player should be able to progress through the game
	- the player would start with a small, basic restaurant and work their way up to a large, successful franchise.
	- Maybe the player could unlock new locations, animatronics, and features as they progress through the game.

6. The player could have a boss that explains the game and gives them objectives to complete.
	- The boss could be a character from the FNAF series, such as William Afton or Henry Emily.
	- The boss could give the player tips and advice on how to run their restaurant.

7. The game could have a day and night cycle.
	- During the day, the player would manage the restaurant and attract customers.
	- During the night, the player would have to defend the restaurant from animatronic attacks.
	- The player could set up security measures, such as cameras and alarms, to protect the restaurant.

8. The game could have a reputation system.
	- The player's reputation would affect the number of customers that visit the restaurant.
	- The player could improve their reputation by providing good service, attracting customers, and completing objectives.
	- The player could lose reputation by providing poor service, losing customers, and failing objectives.

9. Objects in the restaurant could be interactive.
	- An arcade machine could have a mini-game that the player can play to earn extra money.
